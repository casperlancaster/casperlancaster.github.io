ABOUT ME

THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECTS

I'm Clayton Miller—a Game Development student at Full Sail University, the founder of Cabdem Marketing LLC, and someone who took an unusual route into software development.

Before I started building applications, I spent about 15 years as a professional truck driver. That work taught me how to plan ahead, solve problems under pressure, and understand what happens when real-world systems have to work together.

Today, I'm putting those experiences to work in code. TruckzSim brings my trucking background into a C++ management simulation, while my work through Cabdem Marketing includes Python/Flask websites, auction and shopping tools, database-backed workflows, and customer communication features.

I'm working toward my B.S. in Game Development, expected June 2028. I want to keep building believable simulation experiences and dependable software—and get better at every part of the process, from design and debugging to the final user experience.

02 / DEVELOPMENT CHALLENGE

FROM IDEA TO STRUCTURE

The challenge was turning a large simulation into independent, connected systems without breaking existing features. I refactored the original application into focused classes, tested each system as it grew, and resolved Windows-specific compiler and linker errors in the console-color feature.
